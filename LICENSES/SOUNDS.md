# Sounds – sources & licenses (Skeleton Dance)

All sounds are recorded samples released under **CC0 1.0** (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). No synth beeps, no oscillators. `tools/build.mjs` embeds them as base64 into `js/sounds-src.js` so the game also works via `file://`. Credit is not required for CC0 and is given voluntarily (by source ID). The concert-hall room is a generated convolution reverb.

## Music
*Danse macabre*, Op. 40 (Camille Saint-Saëns, 1874) – the composition is in the public domain. The game plays our own simplified arrangement, sequenced note by note with the samples below.

## Orchestra – VSCO 2 Community Edition (CC0 1.0)
https://vis.versilstudios.com/vsco-community.html – trimmed, faded and loudness-matched; the game re-pitches the nearest note.
